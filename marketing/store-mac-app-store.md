## Description

<!-- limit: 4000 -->
```text
Paper is a reader for research papers, built for the Mac. Long PDFs scroll and zoom smoothly, your highlights and notes work offline, and everything syncs with paper.college on the web.

Read any paper without getting stuck.

SMOOTH ON LONG PDFS
• Paper renders pages natively, so scrolling and zooming stay fluid in long papers full of figures and equations.

HIGHLIGHT TEXT AND FIGURES
• Mark a sentence, a single term, or a whole figure.
• Your highlights stay with the paper on every device.

NOTES IN SPLIT VIEW
• Keep your notes, your glossary, or an explanation open beside the page.
• Write as you read, without losing your place.

MADE FOR THE KEYBOARD
• Move through your library and your papers with keyboard shortcuts.

GET UNSTUCK IN PLACE (needs internet)
• Highlight a confusing term to get a plain-English explanation.
• Each one starts with an everyday analogy, then gives the precise definition.
• Some terms come with a short visual explanation.
• Your glossary builds itself as you read.

FOLLOW THE CITATIONS
• Click a citation to see the reference it points to.
• Paper can look for an open-access PDF of a cited paper and add it to your library.

KEEP READING OFFLINE
• Download a paper once and read it anywhere.
• Highlights and notes you make offline are saved on your Mac and sync when you reconnect.
• Explanations, the glossary, citation lookup and visuals need an internet connection.

ONE LIBRARY, EVERYWHERE
• Sign in with Google or email, the same account you use on paper.college.
• Your papers, highlights, notes and glossary sync between your Mac, the web, and your other devices.
• Add papers by pasting a PDF or arXiv link, or by choosing a PDF file.

Questions or feedback? Visit paper.college.
```

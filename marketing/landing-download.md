## 5. Works offline

**Heading:** `Keep reading with no signal`

**Body:**
Download a paper once and it's yours on that device. Read it on a train, a plane, or in a library basement. Add highlights and notes as usual. Paper saves them on the device and syncs them the next time you connect.

**What works offline / needs a connection** (two-column list):

| Works offline | Needs internet |
| --- | --- |
| Reading downloaded papers | Explanations and visuals |
| Text and figure highlights | Glossary and citation lookup |
| Pen highlights (tablets) | Importing a paper by link |
| Writing and editing notes | Signing in the first time |
| Browsing your library | Syncing changes |

**Footnote:** `AI features run on Paper's servers, so they need an internet connection.`

## 6. Sync with the web

**Heading:** `One library, everywhere you read`

**Body:**
Sign in with the same Google or email account you use on paper.college. Your papers, highlights, notes and glossary show up in the app, and changes in the app show up on the web. Start on your laptop and finish on your tablet.

**Small print:** `If you edit the same note on two devices while offline, the most recent change is kept.`

## 7. FAQ (8)

**Is Paper on iPhone, iPad or Linux?**
Not yet. On those devices, you can read in your browser at paper.college.

**Does the Android app work on phones?**
Paper for Android is designed for tablets. On a phone, paper.college in your browser gives you the best experience. **[VERIFY: phone install allowed / blocked in Play device catalog]**

**Do I need an account?**
Yes. The app uses your Paper account to sync your library across devices. Sign in with Google or with your email.

**I used paper.college without signing in. Will my papers show up?**
Sign in on paper.college in the same browser first. Your papers move to your account, and then they appear in the app. **[VERIFY: guest-to-account merge (RPR PR #82) covers papers, highlights and notes]**

**What can I do offline?**
Read papers you've downloaded, highlight, and write notes. Explanations, the glossary, citation lookup and visuals need an internet connection.

**How does sync work?**
Changes sync in the background whenever you're online. If the same note or highlight changed on two devices, the most recent change wins.

**How do I add papers?**
Paste a link to a PDF or arXiv paper, or pick a PDF file from your device. Papers you add on the web show up in the app too.

**Are my papers private?**
Your library is tied to your Paper account. Other users can't see your papers, highlights or notes. **[VERIFY with RLS owner; do not expand beyond this]**

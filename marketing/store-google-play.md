## Full description

<!-- limit: 4000 -->
```text
Paper is a reader for research papers, built for Android tablets. Long PDFs scroll and zoom smoothly, your highlights and notes work offline, and everything syncs with paper.college on the web.

Read any paper without getting stuck.

SMOOTH ON LONG PDFS
• Paper renders pages natively, so scrolling and zooming stay fluid in long papers full of figures and equations.
• Pinch to zoom into a figure, then flick back to the text.

HIGHLIGHT WITH YOUR PEN
• Highlight and mark up with a stylus, the way you would on a printout.
• Highlight text, single terms, or whole figures.
• Your highlights stay with the paper on every device.

NOTES IN SPLIT VIEW
• Keep your notes, your glossary, or an explanation open beside the page.
• Write notes as you read, without losing your place.

GET UNSTUCK IN PLACE (needs internet)
• Highlight a confusing term to get a plain-English explanation.
• Each explanation starts with an everyday analogy, then gives the precise definition.
• Some terms come with a short visual explanation.
• Your glossary builds itself as you read.

FOLLOW THE CITATIONS
• Tap a citation to see the reference it points to.
• Paper can look for an open-access PDF of a cited paper and add it to your library.

KEEP READING OFFLINE
• Download a paper once and read it anywhere: on a train, on a plane, or in a library basement.
• Highlights and notes you make offline are saved on your tablet and sync when you reconnect.
• Explanations, the glossary, citation lookup and visuals need an internet connection.

ONE LIBRARY, EVERYWHERE
• Sign in with Google or email, the same account you use on paper.college.
• Your papers, highlights, notes and glossary sync between the app, the web, and Paper on Windows and Mac.
• Add papers by pasting a PDF or arXiv link, or by picking a PDF file.

Paper for Android is designed for tablets. On a phone, use paper.college in your browser.

Questions or feedback? Visit paper.college.
```

## Description

<!-- limit: 10000 -->
```text
Paper is a reader for research papers, built for Windows. Long PDFs scroll and zoom smoothly, your highlights and notes work offline, and everything syncs with paper.college on the web.

Read any paper without getting stuck.

Smooth on long PDFs
Paper renders pages natively, so scrolling and zooming stay fluid in long papers full of figures and equations.

Highlight text and figures
Mark a sentence, a single term, or a whole figure. Your highlights stay with the paper on every device.

Notes in split view
Keep your notes, your glossary, or an explanation open beside the page. Write as you read, without losing your place.

Made for the keyboard
Move through your library and your papers with keyboard shortcuts.

Get unstuck in place (needs internet)
Highlight a confusing term to get a plain-English explanation. Each one starts with an everyday analogy, then gives the precise definition. Some terms come with a short visual explanation. Your glossary builds itself as you read.

Follow the citations
Click a citation to see the reference it points to. Paper can look for an open-access PDF of a cited paper and add it to your library.

Keep reading offline
Download a paper once and read it anywhere. Highlights and notes you make offline are saved on your PC and sync when you reconnect. Explanations, the glossary, citation lookup and visuals need an internet connection.

One library, everywhere
Sign in with Google or email, the same account you use on paper.college. Your papers, highlights, notes and glossary sync between Windows, the web, and Paper on Android tablets and Mac. Add papers by pasting a PDF or arXiv link, or by choosing a PDF file.

Questions or feedback? Visit paper.college.
```
